Collaborating Physician Agreement

Psychiatric-Mental Health Nurse Practitioner Collaboration · State of Texas · Facilitated by NubridgeMD

Between Ekaete Okon, APRN-PMHNP-BC (Nurse Practitioner) and Muhammad Bilal, MD (Collaborating Physician)

This Collaborating Physician Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into and effective as of November 1, 2026 (the “Effective Date”), by and between Ekaete Okon, APRN-PMHNP-BC, a board-certified Psychiatric-Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (the “Nurse Practitioner” or “NP”), and Muhammad Bilal, MD, a licensed physician (the “Collaborating Physician” or “Physician”). The collaboration is facilitated by NubridgeMD. The NP and the Physician are each a “Party” and together the “Parties.”

1. Purpose

The Physician agrees to serve as the collaborating (or supervising, as the applicable jurisdiction may designate) physician for the Nurse Practitioner’s psychiatric-mental health practice. The Parties enter into this collaboration to support the delivery of safe, high-quality patient care in accordance with the NP’s scope of practice, applicable federal and Texas law, and the rules of the Texas Board of Nursing and the Texas Medical Board.

2. Licensure & Credentials

Each Party represents that they hold, and will maintain throughout the term, a current, active and unrestricted license to practice in the State of Texas (and any other state in which services are provided), together with all certifications, DEA registrations and other registrations required for their role. The Nurse Practitioner is a certified Psychiatric-Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (APRN-PMHNP-BC). Identifying details:

  • Collaborating Physician — Muhammad Bilal, M.D./D.O. · Texas License No. __________ · DEA No. __________

3. Scope of Practice

The Nurse Practitioner will provide psychiatric-mental health services within the scope of her education, national certification, licensure and applicable law — including psychiatric evaluation, diagnosis, medication management, psychotherapy and related behavioral-health services — for the patient population authorized by her national certification (Psychiatric-Mental Health — Across the Lifespan). The NP will practice in accordance with prevailing standards of care and will consult with, and refer to, the Collaborating Physician as clinically appropriate and as required by applicable law.

4. Practice Locations & Telehealth Platforms

The Nurse Practitioner provides, and may provide, services under this Agreement at and through the following practice locations and platforms, including but not limited to:

  • PEP Wellness Center;
  • Headway; and
  • any other telehealth platforms through which Ekaete Okon, APRN-PMHNP-BC currently provides or may provide services in the future.

The foregoing is in each case subject to applicable laws, licensure requirements, scope-of-practice limitations, and the terms of this Collaborating Physician Agreement. The Physician’s collaboration under this Agreement extends to the Nurse Practitioner’s practice across the locations and platforms described above, consistent with the collaboration terms set out herein and the requirements of each applicable jurisdiction. The addition of a new practice location or telehealth platform does not require a separate agreement, but remains governed by this Agreement and by all applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

5. Collaboration & Availability

The Collaborating Physician will be reasonably available to the Nurse Practitioner for consultation and collaboration by telephone or secure electronic means during the NP’s practice hours, and will collaborate on the management of complex or high-acuity cases as clinically indicated or as required by law. The Parties will maintain a means of timely contact and will arrange appropriate coverage during planned absences.

6. Chart Review & Quality

The Collaborating Physician will conduct periodic review of a representative sample of the Nurse Practitioner’s patient records, selected to include prescriptions and higher-risk encounters, and will document such reviews and collaboration at the frequency required by Texas rule. The Parties will cooperate in good faith to meet all quality-assurance, consultation and record-keeping requirements imposed by law or by the relevant Boards.

7. Prescriptive Authority

The Nurse Practitioner may prescribe, order and administer medications and devices within her scope of practice and in accordance with applicable federal and Texas law and the requirements of this collaboration. Any prescribing of controlled substances will occur only where permitted by applicable law and the terms of the collaboration, and in accordance with the NP’s and (where required) the Physician’s DEA registrations.

8. Compliance with Law

Each Party will comply with all applicable federal and Texas laws and regulations, the rules of the Texas Board of Nursing and the Texas Medical Board, the requirements of any telehealth platform or facility through which services are rendered, and all privacy and security requirements, including HIPAA. Services will be delivered consistent with the standards of care for psychiatric-mental health practice.

9. Professional Liability Insurance

Each Party will maintain professional liability (malpractice) insurance in amounts customary for their role and as required by applicable law or by any platform or facility through which services are provided, and will provide evidence of such coverage upon reasonable request.

10. Independent Professionals

Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership or agency relationship between the Parties. Each Party is an independently licensed professional responsible for their own clinical judgment, licensure, taxes and obligations. NubridgeMD acts solely as the platform that facilitates the collaboration and is not a party to the clinical care rendered.

11. Term & Termination

This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date (or on the date of the last signature below, if later) and continues until terminated, and will be reviewed and renewed at least annually or as Texas law requires. Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days’ written notice to the other Party. This Agreement terminates immediately upon the suspension, revocation or material restriction of either Party’s license. Upon termination, the NP will not represent that the collaboration remains in effect, and the Parties will cooperate to transition patient care appropriately.

12. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules, and by all applicable federal law. This is the entire agreement of the Parties; if any provision is unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect.

13. Electronic Signatures

The Parties agree that this Agreement may be executed electronically, and that electronic signatures and records are valid and enforceable to the same extent as handwritten signatures and paper records under the U.S. Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (E-SIGN) and the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (UETA).
